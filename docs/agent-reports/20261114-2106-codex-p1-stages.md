## 7. Can a teammate explain this?

These four pure modules turn each incoming EEG band frame into clean, normalised-ready inputs: `preprocess` rejects invalid numbers and checks if the frame is recent against a caller-supplied clock, `features` computes the resting (alpha-ratio) and active-play (beta-ratio, partly muscle) numbers from the spectrum, `quality` scores contact and packet loss separately from 0 to 1, and `calibration` computes per-player low/high baselines while rejecting unstable or unseparated recordings.
