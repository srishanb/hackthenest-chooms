## 7. Can a teammate explain this?

The game validates section data before drawing it, and static platforms declare whether they are safe recovery anchors. The level checker tries many charge jumps with the same physics the game runs and reports the IDs it cannot reach. It also checks refuge and finish zones rather than assuming a reachable section makes every zone reachable.
