| D-35 | 16 Nov 2026 | Srishan | The endless climb is assembled from set pieces for variety: between two anchors a chunk is a seeded run of short shapes (ladder, staircase, traverse, zigzag shaft, detour down, fork, pillar hops, big leap, ice run, moving crossing), then an approach ladder onto the next anchor. | D-36 | 16 Nov 2026 | Srishan | The endless climb gains three new kinds of terrain, as set pieces: bounce mushrooms (a cap that throws the candle about twice as high as a full jump, keeping its sideways speed), crumbling ledges (hold 400 ms after the first landing, then fall; grow back after 3 s once the candle is clear) and updraft columns (a drift column slows a fall to a 60 px/s sink, a rise column lifts at 150 px/s; half gravity while rising through either). They are game mechanics with fixed rules in `game.config.ts` `ledgeKinds`, never signal-driven; `src/game/player/` still never imports the composure contract. They are simulated inside `stepPlayer` (crumble timers by `stepWorld`, shared by the game and the route replay), so every route that uses them is proved like any other. They unlock up the ramp one at a time (bounce from chunk 5, crumbling 6, updrafts 7 and 8). Wall slide and wall jump come next. |
| D-36a | 17 Nov 2026 | Srishan (lead's call after QA, reversible by the owner) | Addendum to D-36. An updraft column never catches a fall: it holds the candle only while its feet are at most 24 px below where it last took off (`ledgeKinds.updraft.holdBelowTakeoffPx`), so falls stay unbounded (D-33). A crumbling ledge holds 600 ms (was 400; still never long enough for a full charge), and every route step off one leaves a 250 ms reaction before the press (`crumbleReactionMs`), since a key held through the landing does nothing. A fall that ends on a bounce cap counts as a fall at its full height. The classic climb is unchanged. A bounce breaks the flow streak only when the fall onto the cap is at least `composure.fall.minPx`; the small drop onto a cap on the intended route leaves it alone. |
| D-37 | 17 Nov 2026 | Srishan (lead's brief for the owner; a **prototype behind a switch**, off by default) | Wall slide and wall jump, for more variety in movement. Off by default: with the switch off every chunk for every seed is unchanged and the classic climb never meets a wall. `?walls=1` turns it on, in production builds too (so the owner can try it on the preview); wall pieces then appear from chunk 1 and the run never saves a best height. A wall is a platform of type `wall` (solid sides and underside, standable top); only walls grip, never the world's edges. Grip only while the feet are at most 96 px below the last takeoff or kick, so a long fall never slides or kicks (D-33). Slide: holding into the wall, the fall eases to 90 px/s. Kick: a fresh jump press within 6 px throws the candle up and away (620 px/s at 58 degrees, about 100 px of climb, less than half a full jump), ignores held direction for 160 ms, and cannot repeat on the same wall before a landing or another wall. Pieces: wall kick and chimney, every step proved with the real physics from shifted takeoffs and early or late presses. Rules in `game.config.ts` `wallMoves`, never signal-driven. Turning it on for everyone is the owner's call after trying it. Amended 20 Nov 2026 after QA: what is proved is every takeoff 0.25 px apart within 2 px pressed on time, every press up to 6 steps early or late from the stored takeoff, and takeoffs 1 and 2 px either side combined with every press offset (not the takeoffs between those together with a press offset); a wall over a ledge leaves the candle's height plus 4 px under it and never splits a ledge, and the replay refuses a step whose ledge or takeoff a wall cuts into; a landing after kicks measures its fall from where the candle last stood, not from the last kick (the grip still measures from the kick). |
| D-38 | 20 Nov 2026 | Srishan | An online two-player climb: each player on their own laptop with their own camera, the two candles solid to each other (stand on a head, shove on the ground, knock in the air). It is an exception to binding rule 3 for that mode only, chosen by the owner knowing it needs the network and a service to connect the players; solo play still never uses the network. Built in two phases behind a URL switch, off by default ([plan](plans/MP-online-two-player.md)): phase A shares the climb with no network (two windows on one machine), phase B connects two laptops and waits for the owner's choice of service and for the exception's wording in `AGENTS.md`. Each laptop owns its own candle and sends its state; shoves are events; a multiplayer run never saves a best height. |
| D-38a | 20 Nov 2026 | Srishan (lead's calls after QA, reversible by the owner) | Addendum to D-38, phase A. A rider and the candle it stands on never knock each other: the carrier may jump with a rider on its head (its jump is normal; the rider is left behind and falls), and a rider's jump from a head is a normal jump, so the most a pair gains is a head's height plus one jump (about 250 px, pinned by a test). A head carries its rider only as far as a candle can really move in one step; walls, the world's edge and the other candle block a carry like a walk. One contact in the air gives one knock to each candle or to neither; a knock noticed by the other game lands up to 1.5 s late so a candle is never knocked alone. A silent player fades out and comes back when heard again; a reloaded guest replaces its silent self after 1.5 s and is told the host's wax; a handshake gives up after 15 s with a reason. Protocol 2: every number received from the other game is bounded by the local rules, from one table. |
