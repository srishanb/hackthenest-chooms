## Goal

While the recovery ember is activating, offer, never require, closing your eyes, with audio cues so a player who
does can follow progress and know when they are back. The UI says plainly that closing your eyes is not proof of
calmness. Nothing about activation, its target or its rules changes.

## What exists already

- `EmberSnapshot.active` and `progress01` (`src/shared/contracts/game-state.ts`) and the events `ember_returned`,
  `ember_cancelled`, `signal_lost`, `signal_restored` already carry everything the cues need.
- Activation already counts only `restingActive && valid` time (P6.3); the eyes-closed alpha rise feeds the same
  resting feature. P6.1 plans a UI-local `cue-tone.ts` for eyes-closed calibration cues; P10 reuses it.

## Contract changes requested

None. The offer shows on every activation (no `Settings` field); the tones reuse P6.1's cue and its numbers.

## Tasks and acceptance tests

### P10.1 The offer (UI)

1. While `ember.active`, the prompt adds: you may close your eyes; a rising tone follows progress and a chime means
   you are back; closing your eyes is not proof of calmness.

### P10.2 Audio cues

1. With P6.1's cue (no file, no network, `master × sfx` volume), a short tone at each quarter of `progress01`
   while `ember.active`; silent when not active.
2. A chime on `ember_returned`; a low tone on `ember_cancelled` and on `signal_lost` (so a paused count is heard).

### P10.3 Signal check

1. On H-19 recordings, confirm closing the eyes does not hold the artifact gate for more than about 1 s (a blink).
