### P8.4 Visual instability polish (ATM-4, Core polish)

| Part | Numbers (Neil's rows) | Renderer (Ali) |
|---|---|---|
| Lantern flicker | `lanternFlicker` | Varies the light's alpha; never below `lanternRadius.min` |
| Swaying scenery | `backgroundMotion` | Far layers only, e.g. `setScrollFactor` < 1, `TileSprite.tilePositionX` |
| Distant distortion | `backgroundMotion`, low cap | `enableFilters()`, `filters.internal.addDisplacement()`, far only |

Phaser names verified in `phaser.d.ts` 4.2.1 and the filters guide; their look is untested. Displacement is
**never** added to `cameras.main`, which would distort the gameplay view.

1. Enable both rows with small caps: `lanternFlicker.max` at most 0.3, `backgroundMotion.max` at most 0.5.
2. **Reduced motion.** With `reducedMotionScale: 0` the engine targets `neutral` (today 0.1 and 0.2), so a renderer
   reading those as amplitudes would still animate. Both renderers check `settings.reducedMotion` and draw static
   (P5.3's `background.ts` already plans this; P8.4 adds sway and distortion to that file). Neutrals stay.
3. The gameplay camera, platforms and player never move with any effect value.

| Acceptance test | How |
|---|---|
| Off under reduced motion | Browser: reduced motion on; for 10 s nothing flickers, sways or ripples. |
| Camera stable | `?source=sim`, hold clench while standing: platform edges stay at the same screen spot. |
| Caps hold | New `effects-table.test.ts` asserts both caps. |
